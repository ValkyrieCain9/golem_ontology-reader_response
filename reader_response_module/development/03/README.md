# Iteration 3 - Motivating Scenario

## Name
Discourse Acts and Evaluations Acts

## Description
When someone engages with a creative work, contributes to an online forum, posts a comment or review about something, the most basic function of all these acts is to communicate, an idea, opinion, feeling, etc. The nature of this communicaiton is almost always made with a recipient in mind some object or reciever of the communication. In the digital context this could be a comment made to be shared within a community or seen by the creator of a creative work. This exchange of ideas between actors online is what Zhang et al. (2017) define as discourse acts and propose a set of 9 categories with which to define the different types of discourse acts - announcement (i.e statement), question, answer, elboration, agreement, disagreement, appreciation, negative reaction, and humour. They further specify the type of relation that can exist between each category, emphasising the recipricol nature of this type of communicaiton. 

While Zhang et al. (2017) defined these discourse acts for the digital context of online forums (i.e Reddit), their generality makes them suited for several different context and also allows for expansion with more specific taxonony. In particular, we can consider the evalution of creative works (eg literature) as one such expansion. If we consider evaluation as a subcategory of these discourse acts then we can define it as the communication of quality. Rebora and Vezzani (2024), who created a model for literary evaluation disguish between three general types of evaluation, individual, aesthetic and social. Each of these categories can then also act and broad containers for more specific theories related to these different types of evaluation.
