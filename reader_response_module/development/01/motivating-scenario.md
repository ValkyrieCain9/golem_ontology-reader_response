# Iteration 1 - Motivating Scenario

## Name
Comments and Commenting

## Description
A comment is a response to a piece of content posted on the internet. It is often textual, sometimes with visual elements like emojis, images or gifs. Comments do not exist in their own right but are instead linked to the post they are responding to. But a comment can also be responding to and linked to another comment. When multiple replies are nested under a comment this is known as a thread. 

A comment is made by a user who must create a profile account to be able to make a comment on the post's site or platform. The user also has the option to give a rating to comment as a response, based on the rating system of the post's site or platform.
